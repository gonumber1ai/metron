# Ad captions — "no pills, no concoctions"

**Creative:** woman sitting on the edge of the bed, towel, head in her hands. Man standing
a few feet away, dressed or half-dressed, hands slightly out — confused, useless. No faces
needed. Cool light. It should look like a photograph, not an ad.

---

## The rule that decides which tier you can use

Meta and Google reject two things here, and they reject them separately:

1. **Sexual content.** Anything explicit, or an image that reads as post-sex.
2. **Implied personal attributes.** Ads may not say or imply *"you"* have a condition.
   "Do you finish too fast?" is a rejection. "He finishes too fast" is not, because it is
   not addressed at the viewer.

So the paid captions below are written in the **third person**, about a man in a photo. The
viewer does the identifying himself, which is stronger copy anyway — nobody likes being
told what's wrong with them by an ad.

Never in a paid caption: *last longer, stamina, premature, erection, performance, bedroom,
satisfy, in bed*. These are keyword-flagged regardless of context.

---

## TIER A — paid social (Meta, Google, TikTok ads)

Image does the work. Caption never accuses the viewer.

1. **It wasn't the pills. It wasn't the herbs. And it wasn't what she was thinking either.**

2. **He tried everything in the pharmacy.
   The problem was never in the pharmacy.**

3. **Two people in one room, and neither of them saying it.**

4. **Nothing in a bottle was ever going to fix this.
   He found that out at 47.**

5. **He spent four years buying the wrong solution.
   It took ten days to fix the right one.**

6. **Every man in this situation reaches for a bottle first.
   Almost none of them reach for the thing that actually works.**

**Primary text under any of the above:**

> Most men in this situation try three things, in the same order: a pill, a spray, then
> something a friend swore by. All three are rented. Stop paying and it stops working.
>
> There is a fourth thing. It takes 15 minutes a day for 10 days, there is nothing to
> swallow, and if it doesn't work you get your money back.
>
> 9 questions to find out if it applies to you. Nobody sees your answers.

**CTA button:** Learn More
**Headline (40 char):** 10 days. Nothing to swallow.
**Description:** Private. No name needed.

## TIER B — organic social, Reels, TikTok, YouTube

Looser than paid. Still avoid the flagged word list — organic gets suppressed, not rejected,
and suppression is invisible so you never learn.

1. **No pills. No concoctions. No spray.
   10 days, 15 minutes a day. Money back if nothing changes.**

2. **The pharmacy sells you one night at a time.
   Nobody sells you the thing that lasts, because you'd only buy it once.**

3. **She's not thinking what he thinks she's thinking.
   She's thinking "why won't he talk about it."**

4. **Three things men buy for this. All three stop working the day you stop paying.**

## TIER C — WhatsApp status, email, SMS, retargeting

No platform review. Say it plainly.

1. **No pills. No herbs. No spray.
   10 days, 15 minutes a day — and if you're not lasting longer at the end, we refund you.**

2. **You've bought the pills. You've bought the herbs. You're still where you started.
   Try the thing that doesn't need buying twice.**

## Safer creative variants for paid

The towel image will struggle at review. Same emotional beat, far lower rejection risk:

- **Two coffee cups on a table, one untouched.** Man out of focus behind. Reads as distance
  without a single sexual signal.
- **A made bed, morning light, one man sitting on the edge of it with his back to camera,
  fully dressed.** Ambiguous enough to pass, specific enough to land.
- **A bathroom cabinet, open, four different boxes and bottles on the shelf.** Caption:
  *"He tried everything in the pharmacy."* This one is the strongest — the product shot IS
  the objection, and there is no person in it at all to flag.
- **Man sitting on the edge of a bed at night, phone in hand, lit only by the screen.**

Run the cabinet shot as your control. It is the least likely to be rejected and the most
likely to be understood in under a second.

---

## Where these point

All of them go to the **quiz**, never to the offer page. The quiz does the pain and the
personalisation; the offer only makes sense after a man has stated his own two numbers.
Ad → quiz → result → offer.

Run the funnel domain, not `metron.life`, so a flagged ad account never takes the brand
domain down with it.
